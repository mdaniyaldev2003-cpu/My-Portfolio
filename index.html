A quick look into my background

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate (Class of 2027) with a strong specialization in Data Engineering. My expertise lies in designing and implementing real-time streaming pipelines using Apache Kafka and Spark, building automated ETL workflows with Apache Airflow, and managing cloud-based data infrastructure on AWS and Google Cloud.

With hands-on experience in both batch and streaming architectures, I bring a comprehensive understanding of the modern data stack and a commitment to writing clean, maintainable code.
